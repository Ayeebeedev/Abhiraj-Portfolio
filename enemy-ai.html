PROJECT 01

Enemy AI System

A custom enemy AI system developed in Unreal Engine 5 for my horror game prototype, focused on creating believable enemy behavior and player interactions.

AI Perception

The enemy uses Unreal Engine's AI Perception system to detect the player through sight.

Once the player enters the enemy's perception range, the AI can react and begin its investigation or chase behavior.

AI Perception Blueprints Unreal Engine 5

Patrol System

When the player is not detected, the enemy follows predefined patrol points around the environment.

The system allows the enemy to move naturally between different locations instead of remaining in a single area.

Behavior Tree Patrol AI Controller

Investigation

If the enemy notices something suspicious, it can move towards the location and investigate instead of immediately attacking the player.

This creates a more believable behavior and gives the player opportunities to hide or escape.

Investigation Behavior Tree Blackboard

Chase & Attack

After confirming the player's presence, the enemy transitions into a chase state and attempts to catch the player.

The system also handles the transition into the attack behavior when the enemy reaches the player.
